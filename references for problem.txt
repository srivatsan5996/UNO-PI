The Chasnala mining disaster was a disaster that happened on 27 December 1975 in a coal mine in Chasnala near Dhanbad in the Indian state of Jharkhand. An explosion in the mine followed by flooding killed 375 miners.

Absence of Dedicated Rescue Stations in the Quarry Sector: While major lignite operations (NLC India, Neyveli) maintain trained personnel under the Mines Rescue Rules (1985), the thousands of minor mineral quarries operate without statutory rescue stations, leaving emergencies to general civil defence or standard fire services untrained in mining dynamics.
